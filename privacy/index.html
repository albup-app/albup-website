What this policy covers

This draft describes the current AlbUp iOS app, the proposed informational website, and support correspondence. The website is currently a local preview. GitHub Pages hosting is planned but has not been activated.

The final published policy must be checked against the version of the app and website actually released.

Information stored on your device

The current app does not require an online account, email address, date of birth, or password. It stores learning progress, preferences, bookmarks, story completion, Stars and rewards, avatar and Closet choices, dictionary favorites, and an optional nickname on your device. A real name is not required.

Onboarding preferences include interface language, learner category, learning goals, preferred Albanian variety, proficiency, daily goal, and how you heard about AlbUp. The app does not implement its own cloud sync or transmit this local learner information to an AlbUp server. Apple-managed device backups may include app data depending on your settings.

Microphone and pronunciation practice

Recording is optional. AlbUp requests microphone permission when you choose to record in a supported activity. Pronunciation attempts are temporarily stored and processed locally using a bundled Whisper speech-recognition model. The current implementation does not upload these recordings or recognized words to an AlbUp server, OpenAI, or a remote speech-recognition provider.

Recognized words are used to provide feedback during an attempt, not as a long-term transcript history. The app attempts to delete temporary recordings when an attempt is cleared or the pronunciation session closes, and removes stale recordings when a pronunciation session starts. An interruption or failed deletion may leave a file until later cleanup; immediate deletion is not guaranteed.

You can revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings. Other learning activities remain available without recording.

App audio, third-party components, and tracking

Albanian recordings and learning videos are bundled with the app. Some English and German spoken assistance uses Apple’s system speech synthesis. Whisper, whisper.cpp, and ggml components support local pronunciation processing; using these components does not itself send recordings to their authors.

No advertising or analytics SDK, advertising tracking, or remote inference service was identified in the reviewed app source. The app does not request contacts or location. Apple operates the App Store, TestFlight, and operating-system services under its own privacy practices.

Website hosting and browser information

The proposed website consists of static pages with locally served artwork and system fonts. This version contains no contact form, analytics script, advertising, external video embed, cookies set by site code, or browser storage used to remember language. The language switcher uses ordinary page links.

If published using GitHub Pages, GitHub receives requests needed to deliver the website. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, including visits by people who are not signed in. The app’s local processing therefore does not mean website visits are free of third-party data processing. See GitHub Pages data-collection documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support emails

If you email albup.help@outlook.com, the message is sent to an Outlook.com mailbox. Correspondence can include your sender address, name if supplied, message content, attachments, and technical details you choose to provide. Microsoft processes email as part of providing Outlook.com, its consumer email service. This mailbox is not being described as a Microsoft 365 business tenancy. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement, including its Outlook and storage/processing sections. Provider processing can involve countries outside your own; this draft does not promise country-exclusive storage or a particular transfer safeguard for AlbUp.

The purpose of support correspondence is to respond to technical questions, feedback, and privacy requests. Please avoid sending children’s voice recordings or unnecessary sensitive information.

Kaltrina Kabashi-Sylaj is the only person authorized by the developer to access and manage this support mailbox. Microsoft’s processing as the email provider is separate from this access arrangement.

Resolved support emails and their attachments are retained for up to 12 months after the issue is resolved, unless they are needed longer for an ongoing matter or legal obligation. Kaltrina Kabashi-Sylaj manages and deletes these emails manually. Deletion from the mailbox does not promise immediate erasure from all provider backups or systems.

Retention and control

Local learning information remains so you can continue between sessions. Changing a nickname or preferences updates those settings. Deleting the app removes its local app container; offloading retains documents and data, and device backups may retain or restore information. There is no AlbUp server-side learner account to delete.

The support-email retention rule is described above. Hosting-provider retention is separate from app storage and remains subject to the hosting review.

Children and family use

AlbUp is a general-audience educational app intended for children, teenagers, adults, and families, with worldwide release planned. Learners can use AlbUp without an account or real name. Parents and guardians can manage device permissions and review local learner settings. Parents or guardians may contact the support address with privacy questions.
