Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. AlbUp does not require an online account.

Can I learn Albanian as a beginner?

Yes! AlbUp offers step-by-step lessons to help learners build their Albanian vocabulary and language skills.

Can I practice pronunciation?

Yes. Supported activities let you listen to Albanian audio and practice speaking.

How do I enable my microphone?

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, then enable AlbUp. If AlbUp isn’t listed, open Speak & Repeat and tap “Tap to Speak” to request access. Labels may vary with your iOS version.
